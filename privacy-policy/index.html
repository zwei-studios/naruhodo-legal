Privacy Policy for Naruhodo

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Naruhodo ("App", "we", "us", or "our") is a Japanese-learning iOS application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and which third parties receive it when you use the App or the website at naru-app.com.

If you do not agree with these practices, please do not use the App or the website.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Learning data is stored locally on your device (and, if you enable it, in your personal iCloud account). We do not operate a user-account system.

  • Learning preferences: difficulty range, enabled shows, disabled episodes, onboarding answers
  • Saved vocabulary, grammar, and favorites
  • Progress: seen clips, review/SRS state, streaks, unlock progress
  • Optional iCloud backup: a snapshot of vocabulary, favorites, selected settings, and seen-clip counts, stored in your iCloud account via Apple. We cannot read this backup from our servers.

3. Information We Send Off-Device

3.1 Product analytics (PostHog)

The App uses PostHog (EU cloud, eu.i.posthog.com) to understand how the App is used and to improve it. We send:

  • App lifecycle and screen views
  • Product events (for example clip views, vocabulary saves, reviews, paywall and onboarding steps, downloads, backups)
  • An anonymous RevenueCat app user ID so analytics can be joined with subscription status
  • If you submit in-app feedback: the message text and, if you provide it, a contact email. Email is optional and only used to reply.

PostHog may also receive standard device and request metadata (such as IP address, device type, OS version, and language) as part of operating the SDK. See PostHog's Privacy Policy.

3.2 Subscriptions (RevenueCat and Apple)

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and subscription status. RevenueCat receives purchase receipts, product identifiers, subscription status, and an anonymous app user ID. The App sends its AppsFlyer ID to RevenueCat; RevenueCat then sends configured subscription-lifecycle events and associated purchase data to AppsFlyer server-to-server. RevenueCat's automatic collection of advertising and vendor identifiers is disabled. If you arrived via Apple Ads, we send Apple's AdServices attribution token to RevenueCat so we can see which campaigns led to trials and paid subscriptions. This does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

3.3 Advertising measurement (AppsFlyer, Meta, and TikTok)

The App includes the AppsFlyer Strict SDK — not the Meta or TikTok SDKs — so we can measure whether ads led to installs, app sessions, trial starts, and purchases, and improve our campaigns. AppsFlyer may receive:

  • App install, launch, and session events, together with app and device metadata
  • An AppsFlyer-generated device identifier, Identifier for Vendor (IDFV), IP address, user agent, OS and app version, and App Tracking Transparency status
  • Subscription-lifecycle and purchase events sent server-to-server by RevenueCat, including the anonymous app user ID, product identifier, amount, and currency where applicable

Through the partner integrations we configure in AppsFlyer, AppsFlyer sends selected install, session, trial, and purchase postbacks and permitted event data to Meta and TikTok for advertising measurement and optimization. This can include postbacks for events that AppsFlyer did not attribute to that partner. We do not collect or share the advertising identifier (IDFA), do not include the Meta or TikTok SDKs in the App, and do not currently show an App Tracking Transparency prompt. Apple may also provide aggregated SKAdNetwork measurement that does not identify you through IDFA. See AppsFlyer's Privacy Policy, Meta's Privacy Policy, and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

3.4 Content delivery (Cloudflare)

Video clips and content files are downloaded from our content servers on Cloudflare. Those requests include standard HTTP data (IP address, device type, user agent). See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

3.5 Website (naru-app.com)

The marketing site is a Cloudflare Worker. Cloudflare sees standard request data (including IP address) to deliver the site. For tracked links (for example /r/<code>), we store only the campaign code, event kind, country code (cf-ipcountry), and user agent — not the IP address. The website does not currently load a Meta Pixel or similar browser advertising pixel.

3.6 Instagram comment-to-DM (our servers)

If you comment a campaign keyword on one of our Instagram accounts (currently Naru, Riley, or Rhea), Meta sends us a webhook. We store that event in our own database so we can send at most one private reply with a tracked App Store link and debug failures. Stored fields include:

  • webhook_events: Instagram-scoped commenter ID, comment text, media ID, our account ID, and the complete Meta webhook payload (raw JSON)
  • dm_log: comment ID, recipient (Instagram-scoped user ID), media ID, matched keyword, link code, and send status

We keep these rows to operate and audit the feature (dedupe, one DM per person per post, failure diagnosis). They are not used to advertise to you in the App. Meta also processes the same comments and DMs under Instagram's Privacy Policy; that does not replace this disclosure of what we store.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require an account or registration
  • We do not collect your name unless you choose to put it in optional feedback
  • We do not collect your email unless you type it into optional in-app feedback
  • We do not collect the advertising identifier (IDFA)
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, photos, or contacts

5. How We Use This Information

  • Provide the App: personalize the feed, save vocabulary, run reviews, unlock content, process subscriptions, restore iCloud backups
  • Analytics: understand feature use, onboarding, and paywall performance
  • Advertising measurement: measure Meta and TikTok ad performance through AppsFlyer (installs, sessions, trials, purchases) so we can run and improve ads
  • Support: read optional feedback you send
  • Outreach measurement: count website clicks on tracked links

We do not sell your personal data.

6. Advertising Platforms and SKAdNetwork

Meta and TikTok receive only the partner postbacks and event data configured in AppsFlyer; neither advertising platform has its own SDK in the App. Apple may provide aggregated SKAdNetwork conversion measurement, and AppsFlyer may manage the conversion-value schema used for that reporting. These signals are used for campaign measurement and optimization without IDFA.

7. Data Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete the App or clear its data
  • iCloud backup: until you delete the backup or your iCloud data, subject to Apple's retention
  • PostHog, RevenueCat, AppsFlyer, Meta, TikTok, Cloudflare, and website logs: according to each provider's retention and our need to operate analytics, subscriptions, ads measurement, and the site
  • Feedback: kept as long as needed to review it and, if you left contact details, to reply
  • Instagram comment-to-DM logs: kept as long as needed to operate, dedupe, and debug the feature; email us to request deletion

8. Data Security

  • On-device data uses iOS platform storage and encryption
  • Network traffic uses HTTPS
  • iCloud backups use Apple's iCloud security

9. Your Privacy Rights

9.1 Access and deletion

You can delete on-device App data by uninstalling the App or clearing it in iOS Settings. iCloud backups can be removed in iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud (the App has no in-app delete-backup control). For analytics, subscription, ads-measurement, feedback, website click logs, or Instagram comment-to-DM logs held by us or our processors, email salahedine-youssef@outlook.de.

9.2 Tracking and advertising

We do not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt and we do not collect IDFA. We send advertising-measurement events through AppsFlyer, which may provide configured postbacks to Meta and TikTok, and we send Apple Ads attribution tokens to RevenueCat. iOS Settings > Privacy & Security still controls system-level tracking and Apple Ads preferences.

9.3 Age

The App is intended for users 13 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you may request to know, delete, or correct personal information, and you may opt out of sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell personal information for money. Some advertising-measurement disclosures through AppsFlyer to Meta and TikTok may be "sharing" under California law. To exercise these rights, contact us (section 12).

11. European Privacy Rights (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EU/EEA or UK, we process personal data on these bases:

  • Contract / steps prior to contract: providing the App, content delivery, subscriptions
  • Legitimate interests: product analytics, preventing abuse, measuring ads so we can run the App as a business
  • Consent: optional feedback contact details; any future App Tracking Transparency prompt if we add one

You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection, and the right to complain to a supervisory authority. Contact us (section 12).
